# Emissions and Fuel-Efficient Driving

## Low-emission zones

Many city centres are now low-emission zones where older, more polluting vehicles must pay a daily charge or are banned during certain hours. Check your vehicle's emission class before driving into one to avoid a penalty.

## Smooth driving saves fuel

Gentle acceleration and reading the road ahead to avoid needless braking can cut fuel use noticeably. Harsh acceleration followed by hard braking simply turns fuel into wasted heat at the brakes.

## Idling wastes fuel

Leaving the engine running while parked, known as idling, burns fuel and pollutes for no benefit; if you expect to wait more than a minute or two, switch the engine off. Many towns now fine drivers for unnecessary idling near schools.

## Tyre pressure and economy

Correctly inflated tyres roll with less resistance, so keeping them at the recommended pressure improves fuel economy as well as safety. A soft tyre makes the engine work harder to cover the same distance.

# Tolls and Lane Use

## How tolls are charged

On most motorways you take a ticket on entry and pay at the exit, where the fee is calculated from the distance travelled and the vehicle class. Some sections use a flat-rate toll collected at a single barrier.

## Electronic toll tags

An electronic toll tag mounted on the windscreen lets you pass through dedicated lanes without stopping; the fee is debited automatically each month. These lanes are marked with an orange "t" sign.

## Lane discipline

Keep to the right-hand lane and use the left lanes only to overtake. Returning to the right after overtaking is mandatory; cruising in the middle or left lane when the right lane is free is an offence.

## Hard shoulder running

On some congested motorways the hard shoulder is opened as a temporary running lane during peak hours. It may only be used when an overhead gantry shows a green arrow above it; a red cross means it is closed.

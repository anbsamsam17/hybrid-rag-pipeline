# Sharing the Road with Motorcycles

## Checking blind spots

Motorcycles are narrow and easily hidden in a car's blind spot, so make a final over-the-shoulder check before changing lanes or pulling out. A mirror alone is not enough to be sure the space is clear.

## Filtering traffic

In slow or stationary queues a motorcycle may filter between lanes of traffic. Expect it, and check your mirrors before opening a door or changing lane, because a rider passing close alongside can appear very quickly.

## Giving extra room

Give a motorcyclist as much space as you would a car when overtaking, and more in windy weather, because a gust or a poor road surface can push a rider off line without warning.

## Vulnerable road users generally

Cyclists, pedestrians and horse riders are grouped as vulnerable road users because they have no metal shell for protection. Pass them slowly and widely, and be ready for sudden movements near junctions and crossings.

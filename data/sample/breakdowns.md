# Breakdowns and Emergencies

## Stopping on the hard shoulder

If your vehicle breaks down, pull onto the hard shoulder as far to the right as possible and switch on your hazard warning lights immediately. The hard shoulder is for emergencies only; stopping there for a rest or a phone call is forbidden.

## Leaving the vehicle

All occupants must leave the vehicle by the doors away from traffic and wait behind the safety barrier, never inside the car and never on the carriageway. Each person should put on a high-visibility vest before stepping out.

## The warning triangle

Place a warning triangle at least 30 metres behind the vehicle so that approaching drivers are alerted in time. On a fast motorway, position it even further back to give more warning distance.

## Calling for help

Use an orange emergency telephone, found every 2 kilometres along the motorway, rather than a mobile phone: it locates you automatically and connects you to the motorway operator. If you must use a mobile, call the European emergency number 112.

# Road Signs

## Shapes and meaning

A round sign gives an order: a red ring forbids, a blue disc obliges. A triangular sign with a red border is a warning of danger ahead. A square or rectangular sign gives information such as directions or services.

## Colour coding of direction signs

On direction signs, a blue background indicates a motorway, a green background a major through road, and a white background an ordinary local road. Following the colour helps you stay on the intended class of route.

## Temporary roadworks signs

Signs for roadworks use a yellow background instead of the usual white. A yellow temporary sign always overrides the permanent sign it contradicts, because it reflects the current state of the road.

## End-of-restriction sign

A circular sign with a diagonal grey stripe cancels a previous restriction, such as a speed or overtaking limit. After it, the normal default rules apply again.

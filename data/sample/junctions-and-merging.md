# Junctions and Merging

## Joining from a slip road

When you join a motorway from a slip road, use the acceleration lane to match the speed of the traffic already on the carriageway, then merge into a safe gap. Traffic on the main carriageway has priority and should not be forced to brake for you.

## Adjusting your speed to merge

The aim is to arrive at the end of the slip road travelling at about the same speed as the flow you are joining, so you slot in smoothly. Merging much slower than the traffic is a common cause of collisions at entry points.

## Leaving at an exit

Move into the left-hand lane in good time and use the deceleration lane, not the main carriageway, to slow down for the exit. Countdown markers of three, two and one bars warn you the exit is approaching.

## Lane closures and the zip merge

Where a lane is closed ahead, the fair and efficient method is the zip merge: drivers use both lanes until the merge point and then take turns to join, one from each lane. Merging too early wastes road space and lengthens the queue.

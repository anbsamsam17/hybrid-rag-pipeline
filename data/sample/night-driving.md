# Driving at Night

## Using main beam

Use main-beam (full) headlights on unlit roads to see further, but dip them whenever a vehicle approaches or when you are following another car closely, so you do not blind the other driver.

## Coping with oncoming glare

If an oncoming vehicle dazzles you, slow down and look toward the left edge of the road rather than at the lights, keeping the verge line in view until the vehicle has passed. Never retaliate by leaving your own main beam on.

## Overdriving your headlights

Driving faster than the distance your lights reveal is called overdriving your headlights: by the time an obstacle appears in the beam, it is too late to stop. Keep your speed within the lit distance ahead.

## Tiredness after dark

The body clock makes the early hours the hardest time to stay awake, so the risk of falling asleep at the wheel peaks between about 2 a.m. and 6 a.m. Plan long journeys to avoid driving through that window.

# Vehicle Checks Before a Journey

## Minimum tyre tread

The tread on a car tyre must be at least 1.6 millimetres deep across the central three-quarters of the tyre. Below that the tyre grips poorly in the wet and is illegal, risking a fine and penalty points per tyre.

## Checking tyre pressure

Check tyre pressure when the tyres are cold, using the figures in the vehicle handbook, and raise the pressure when carrying a heavy load or several passengers. Under-inflated tyres wear faster and can overheat at speed.

## Lights and washers

Before setting off, confirm that headlights, brake lights and indicators all work, and that the windscreen washer bottle is topped up. A burnt-out brake light hides your braking from the driver behind.

## The fuel and fluid glance

A quick under-bonnet glance at oil and coolant levels, plus a look at the fuel gauge, prevents the most common roadside breakdowns. Running out of fuel on a motorway can itself attract a penalty for a needless stop.

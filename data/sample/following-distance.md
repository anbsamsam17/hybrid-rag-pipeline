# Safe Following Distance

## The two-second rule

Keep at least a two-second gap behind the vehicle in front. Pick a fixed point such as a bridge; when the car ahead passes it, you should not reach the same point before counting two seconds.

## Doubling the gap in the wet

In rain, fog or on a slippery surface, double the gap to at least four seconds, because braking distances grow sharply when grip is reduced. The drier the road, the shorter the safe gap can be.

## Distance markers

Many motorways paint chevrons on the surface; keeping two chevrons between you and the car ahead corresponds roughly to a safe dry-weather gap. Roadside marker posts also help estimate distance.

## Tailgating penalties

Driving too close to the vehicle in front, known as tailgating, is an offence punishable by a fine and licence points, because it is a leading cause of motorway pile-ups.

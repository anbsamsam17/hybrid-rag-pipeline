# Speed Limits on Highways

## Default motorway limit

On a dry motorway the default maximum speed for cars is 130 km/h. This is the baseline limit when no other sign overrides it and conditions are normal.

## Lower limit in rain

In rain or other wet conditions the motorway limit for cars drops to 110 km/h. Drivers must respect the lower figure as soon as the road surface is wet, even if no temporary sign is present.

## Limit for towing and heavy vehicles

A car towing a trailer is capped at 90 km/h on the motorway, regardless of the posted limit for ordinary cars. Heavy goods vehicles over 3.5 tonnes are limited to 90 km/h as well.

## Newly licensed drivers

A driver who has held a licence for less than three years is limited to 110 km/h on a motorway, even in dry weather. The reduced limit applies for the full probationary period.

## Minimum speed

The left-most lane of a motorway has a minimum speed of 80 km/h in good conditions. A vehicle unable to maintain this speed must not use the fast lane.
